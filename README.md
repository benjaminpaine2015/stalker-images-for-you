Prerequisite: This guide requires the Blockbuster Studio (BBS) mod on a desktop version of Minecraft (mobile is not supported).

Create a new film project: Open the Blockbuster menu, navigate to Replays, and click Add Replay.

Spawn the billboard: Click on your newly created replay, select Pick, choose the Billboard (the pink and blue flat 2D model), and press Escape to exit the menu.

Open the editor: Go back into Replays, click on your billboard model, and select Edit.

Apply custom textures: Click Choose a texture, open the folder icon in the top right corner, and drop your image files directly into that folder.
